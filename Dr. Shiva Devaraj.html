Approach to Care

Pediatric care grounded in evidence, delivered with warmth.

Dr. Shiva completed his MD in Pediatrics from Kempegowda Institute of Medical Sciences in Bangalore, and has worked in reputed hospitals managing newborn care, pediatric infections, vaccination, growth and development, and preventive child healthcare.

  • Evidence-based treatment with early diagnosis and preventive care.
  • Active contributor to pediatric academia through research papers and conference presentations.
  • Practical, reassuring guidance for parents on nutrition, vaccination, and everyday child health.

Research and academic contribution.

Dr. Shiva has contributed to pediatric academic literature, including work on early neonatal outcomes in late preterms, meconium aspiration syndrome, clinical profile of childhood pneumonia, and hypomagnesemia as a predictor of pediatric ICU outcomes (PRISM score), among others. He is a member of the Indian Academy of Pediatrics and actively participates in CME programs and pediatric conferences.
