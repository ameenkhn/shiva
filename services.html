Pediatrics

Child care led by Dr. Shiva Devaraj

From the first newborn examination to school-age reviews, the pediatric pathway focuses on early diagnosis, timely immunization, gentle examinations, and clear parental guidance.

  • Newborn and neonatal care with preterm baby follow-up and breastfeeding support.
  • National Immunization Schedule (IAP), optional vaccines, and travel vaccination advice.
  • Growth monitoring, nutrition counselling, and developmental milestone checks.

Mother and child, cared for together — start to finish.

When both doctors work in the same practice, a pregnancy becomes a delivery, a delivery becomes a newborn visit, and a newborn visit becomes years of continuous pediatric follow-up. Nothing is handed off or restarted.

  • Obstetrician and pediatrician share notes when it helps the family.
  • Newborns receive their first examination from the pediatrician immediately after delivery.
  • Parents can book maternal and child visits on the same day when convenient.
