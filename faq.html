Clinic & Services

FAQ

What services does Prabhat Women & Children Clinic offer?
The clinic provides obstetrics and gynaecology care led by Dr. Suguna Nampally, and pediatric care led by Dr. Shiva Devaraj — covering pregnancy, infertility, menstrual and menopausal health, newborn care, vaccinations, growth monitoring, and adolescent health.
Where is the clinic located?
Consultations and admissions are at Prabhat Women & Children Clinic, BTM Layout, Bangalore - 560076.
What are the clinic's working hours?
Consultations are by appointment, Monday to Saturday. Please call to confirm the doctor's OPD timing on your preferred day.
Do you handle high-risk pregnancies?
Yes. Dr. Suguna Nampally has over 30 years of experience managing high-risk pregnancies, including close antenatal follow-up and cesarean deliveries when medically needed.
Can you help with infertility concerns?
Yes. The fertility pathway includes evaluation, ovulation induction, follicular monitoring, preconception counselling, and referral support for assisted reproductive techniques (IUI/IVF) when appropriate.
What pediatric services are available?
Newborn & neonatal care, vaccination, growth & development monitoring, pediatric infections, nutrition & feeding counselling, respiratory and allergy care, and adolescent health are all part of the pediatric pathway.
Do you follow the IAP vaccination schedule?
Yes. Dr. Shiva Devaraj follows the IAP/National Immunization Schedule and also offers optional and travel vaccines when recommended.
Can I bring both my pregnancy and my child's check-up to the same visit?
Yes — when schedules align, you can book mother and child consultations on the same day. Call the clinic to coordinate both appointments.
Do you offer Pap smear and cervical screening?
Yes. Preventive gynaecological screening, Pap smears, breast awareness, and follow-up for abnormal findings are part of Dr. Suguna's routine services.

Appointments & Visits

How do I book an appointment?
Call +91 86183 62859 or send an email to care@prabhatclinic.in. Appointments are confirmed during clinic hours.
What should I bring to my first antenatal visit?
Please bring any previous pregnancy records, ultrasound reports, blood test reports, and a list of current medications. Keep a note of your last menstrual period and any symptoms.
What do I bring for a pediatric visit?
For a first pediatric visit, bring the vaccination booklet (if available), birth record, growth chart, and any prior prescriptions or reports.
Is parking available at the hospital?
Yes, The clinic and the building offer patient parking. Please allow a few extra minutes during peak hours.
