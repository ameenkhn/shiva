Approach to Care

Women's health care that balances experience, precision, and warmth.

Over three decades of practice, Dr. Suguna has guided thousands of women through antenatal care, safe deliveries, infertility work-ups, and every stage of gynaecological health. Her clinical approach focuses on careful history, thoughtful examination, and treatment plans tailored to each patient's life stage and personal needs.

  • Strong preference for safe, normal vaginal deliveries whenever clinically appropriate.
  • Skilled handling of high-risk pregnancies and cesarean sections when medically required.
  • Continuous follow-up from first antenatal visit to postnatal recovery — and back, for future pregnancies.
